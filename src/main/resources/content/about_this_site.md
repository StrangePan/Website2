# About This Site

**My space, my rules.** This site is a place for me to share my passions,
projects, and eventually my thoughts free of third-party interference. It's
a playground for me and my hobbies. 

**No cookies, no spyware, no bloated JavaScript.**
Just you, your browser, and the content. It's no secret that most 
websites are way more complicated than they ought to be.
You don't need a burdensome runtime framework or clunky CMS to build a
decent-looking website if you know a little bit of HTML and CSS.
And you don't have to compromise on aesthetics to get good performance.

**Take a look for yourself.** This website's source code is publicly available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/StrangePan/Website2). It's licensed under the MIT license, so feel free to fork it
or use its styles as a template for your own site.
