# About This Site

**My space, my rules.** This site is a place for me to share my passions,
projects, and eventually my thoughts free of third-party interference. This
place is a playground for me and my hobbies. 

**No cookies, no spyware, no bloated JavaScript.**
Just you, your browser, and the content. It is my opinion that the
state-of-the-art of web development is way more complicated than it ought to be.
You don't need a burdensome runtime framework or clunky CMS to build a
decent-looking website if you already know how to style things with CSS and HTML.
And you don't have to compromise on aesthetics either.

**Take a look for yourself.** This website's source code is publicly available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/StrangePan/Website2) and is licensed under the MIT license.
So feel free to fork it or use its styles as a template for your own site.
